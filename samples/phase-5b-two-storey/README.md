# Phase 5B two-storey SVG samples

These four deterministic schematic sheets are regenerated from the Phase 5A two-storey residential model fixture: 12 m × 10 m footprint, two floors, 3.2 m floor-to-floor height, and a 2.4 m × 3.6 m stair core at (4 m, 3 m).

The ground- and first-floor plans now render every room and room label, every model wall using its explicit thickness, every model door and window from its recorded endpoints, and the per-floor stair-core footprint. The plans do not infer or add model geometry. The elevation and stair-core section retain the Phase 4B renderer output because Phase 5B changes the floor-plan sheets only.

Files: `ground-floor.svg`, `first-floor.svg`, `elevation.svg`, and `stair-core-section.svg`.

The validation banner and title block are retained. Missing measured stair-headroom evidence remains failed; escape-route and regulatory checks remain not_checked. All sheets are schematic design only, not for construction. Placeholder dimensions are not code requirements and these samples do not establish structural, fire, accessibility, regulatory or professional approval.
