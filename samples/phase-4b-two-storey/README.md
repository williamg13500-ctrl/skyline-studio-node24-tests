# Phase 4B two-storey SVG samples

These four deterministic schematic sheets use the Phase 4A test input: a 12 m × 10 m footprint, two floors, 3.2 m floor-to-floor height, residential use, and a 2.4 m × 3.6 m stair core at (4 m, 3 m).

Files: `ground-floor.svg`, `first-floor.svg`, `elevation.svg`, and `stair-core-section.svg`.

The visible banner lists every failed or `not_checked` validator check for the sample model. Missing measured stair headroom, continuous stair-clearance evidence, escape-route graph, egress ruleset screening, vertical-core regulatory screening and core-circulation graph are not treated as verified.

These are sample outputs only. They are not construction documents, code-compliance evidence, or professional approval.
