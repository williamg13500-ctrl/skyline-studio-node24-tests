Owner setup checklist

These connections require your provider accounts and business decisions. Keys belong in Hatchable Setup, never in chat or source files.

Current launch readiness

Checking owner access…

1. Connect language interpretation
In Hatchable’s project Setup, connect an OpenAI, Anthropic or Google provider. The guided reader remains available without it.
2. Connect the rendering worker
The project includes agent.py and scene.py. Run them on a separate host with Python 3, Blender 4.5+ and ffmpeg. Set SKYLINE_ORIGIN and SKYLINE_RENDER_SECRET through the host’s secret manager; save the same secret as RENDER_WORKER_SECRET in Hatchable Setup. Test with python agent.py --once, then enable the render queue in Configure and run the worker as a supervised service.
Jobs use signed requests, expiring leases, revision matching and private video storage. The worker renders the actual model with physical lighting and material colours. Detailed interiors, textures, landscaping and collision-checked walking camera paths are still needed for photorealistic interior walkthroughs.
3. Establish professional review
Appoint qualified architectural, structural and regulatory reviewers for the project’s jurisdiction. Define identity verification, licence checks, scope, applicable code edition, engineering inputs, signed evidence, revision binding and approval revocation. Current requests are private intake records only; they are not sent to a professional and cannot unlock construction exports.
Payments deferred · final implementation phase
Payments are intentionally postponed. Complete the design tools and professional-review foundations first.
  1. Connect a Stripe test secret key in Setup. Create a recurring Studio price and enter its price ID in Configure.
  2. Register /api/billing/webhook on this site for checkout.session.completed and customer.subscription.created, updated and deleted. Use API version 2025-03-31.basil. Add its signing secret in Setup.
  3. Configure the Stripe customer portal, cancellation behaviour and policy links. Test checkout, duplicate delivery, renewal, failed payment and cancellation.
  4. Provide the legal entity, address, jurisdiction, support/privacy contact, prices/taxes, cancellation/refund rules, retention/deletion periods, processors and international transfer details to qualified legal review. Existing terms/privacy pages are drafts.
  5. Publish reviewed terms, privacy and refund URLs in Configure. Confirm policy approval and enable billing only after test-mode checks succeed.
